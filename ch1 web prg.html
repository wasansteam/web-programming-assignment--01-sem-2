Crispy Broast

I've tied maiking chicken many times but this is the best way to make it.

What you'll need

  • 1 kg Chicken pieces
  • 1 cup Yogurt
  • 2 cups Flour and 1/2 cup Cornstarch
  • Ginger-garlic paste
  • Spices: Paprika, chili powder, white pepper, and salt
  • little Oil just to spray for using airfry

How to cook it

  1. Cut slits in the chicken so the flavor gets inside.
  2. Mix yogurt with half the spices and marinate for 4+ hours.
  3. Mix the flour starch and the rest of the spices in a bowl.
  4. Air fry at 170°C for about 12-15 mins.
