Breaking Bad

Overview

It is set in Albuquerque, New Mexico, the show follows Walter White a struggling high school chemistry teacher diagnosed with terminal lung cancer. Together with his ex student Jesse Pinkman Walt turns to a life of crime by producing and selling crystal meth to make money for his family before he dies. As they build their illegal drug empire Walt faces dangerous cartel drug lords, underworld bosses, and even his own brother in law who works for the DEA. The story shows how a normal family man slowly changes into a cold criminal mastermind.

Show Information

Created by: Vince Gilligan

Network: AMC | Original Run: 2008 – 2013

The Cast

  • Bryan Cranston (Walter White)
  • Aaron Paul (Jesse Pinkman)
  • Anna Gunn (Skyler White)
  • Dean Norris (Hank Schrader)

Characters

Walter White: A genius chemist turned into drug lord known as Heisenberg.

Jesse Pinkman: A small-time meth cook who becomes Walt's business partner and moral compass.

Saul Goodman: A "criminal" lawyer who helps the duo navigate the underworld.

Awards & Achievements

During its run, the series received widespread critical acclaim and won numerous awards:

  • 16 Emmy Awards (including Outstanding Drama Series)
  • 2 Golden Globe Awards
  • A Guinness World Record as the highest-rated TV show of all time
